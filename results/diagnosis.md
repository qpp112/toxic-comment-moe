## 4. Validation vs test

Over all 24 BERT-base runs, mean ROC-AUC is 0.0061 lower on the test set than on the validation split (std over runs 0.0003). Per label: `toxic` +0.0137, `severe_toxic` +0.0024, `obscene` +0.0112, `threat` -0.0000, `insult` +0.0091, `identity_hate` +0.0001. A gap that is the same for every head and loss is not something a different head can close.
